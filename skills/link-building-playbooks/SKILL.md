# Link-building playbooks

Pick one playbook per campaign. Each ends with a short prospect list and a drafted email per prospect.

## 1. Broken link replacement
Find pages in the niche linking to dead resources (search "<topic> resources", "<topic> useful links"; fetch candidate pages and check outbound links for 404s). Offer the user's page as the replacement. Best when the user has a page that covers the dead resource's topic.

## 2. Unlinked mentions
Search the web for the brand name excluding the user's own domain. For pages mentioning the brand without a link, ask for the mention to be linked. Highest reply rate of any playbook.

## 3. Outdated statistics
Find well-linked articles quoting old numbers in the niche. Create or point to a page with current, sourced figures (`statistics-page`) and offer the update.

## 4. Resource pages
Search `"<topic>" inurl:resources` and `"<topic>" "helpful links"`. Pitch the single best page for that list, not the homepage.

## 5. AI-citation listicles
Use `find_citation_prospects` (see `ai-citation-outreach`) — pages AI engines cite are the most valuable links to earn now.

## For every playbook

- Prospect table: page, why it fits, contact (from public pages only).
- One specific email per prospect: reference the exact page, the exact fix or addition, and why it helps their reader. Under 120 words.
- The user sends the emails. Never send outreach without explicit instruction.
